# 90-day TAM adoption plan

## Days 1–30: Align and baseline

### Customer outcomes

- Confirm the customer’s priority risks, critical services and regulatory drivers.
- Agree executive sponsor, technical owners and escalation paths.
- Establish the current deployment, support and platform-health baseline.
- Define a joint success plan with measurable 90-day outcomes.

### TAM activities

- Stakeholder and operating-model workshop.
- Review workload coverage, labels, policy maturity and open support cases.
- Identify crown-jewel applications and first enforcement candidate.
- Establish weekly technical cadence and monthly sponsor review.

### Exit evidence

- Signed-off success plan.
- Risk and dependency register.
- Baseline adoption and health scorecard.
- Agreed first application wave.

## Days 31–60: Validate and adopt

### Customer outcomes

- Improve asset ownership and label completeness.
- Validate critical application dependencies.
- Model least-privilege rules with application owners.
- Close high-impact health and support issues.

### TAM activities

- Facilitate dependency and policy-review sessions.
- Coordinate product, Support and Professional Services when specialist input is required.
- Track enablement gaps and arrange targeted training.
- Prepare enforcement-readiness and rollback checklist.

### Exit evidence

- Approved policy matrix.
- Documented exceptions and owners.
- Application-owner validation.
- Enforcement go/no-go decision.

## Days 61–90: Operationalise and demonstrate value

### Customer outcomes

- Complete staged enforcement for the agreed application boundary.
- Establish repeatable health, change and exception processes.
- Demonstrate risk reduction and operational confidence.
- Agree the next adoption and maturity wave.

### TAM activities

- Monitor enforcement outcomes, blocked flows and support trends.
- Lead lessons-learned review and update the rollout method.
- Deliver an executive impact review and first QBR.
- Build the next-quarter adoption roadmap.

### Exit evidence

- Critical application ring-fenced without material service disruption.
- Reduced undocumented or unnecessary flows.
- Operational runbook and ownership accepted.
- Next-quarter roadmap linked to customer priorities.
